Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

This policy explains what ByWhateverMedium L.L.C. (“we”, “us”) collects through Radial Keys (“the app”) and this website, and what we do with it. The short version: the app doesn't send us anything about you, and when you buy it we keep only what we need to get it to you.

The app

Your wheel, settings, presets and any pictures you add stay on your computer. The app has no account, no analytics and no tracking. It only goes online when you drop a preset from this website onto it, to download that preset, and, if we turn it on, to check now and then for a new-version notice. Like any web request, those reach our website with your IP address, and the version check also sends the app's version number.

Buying it

Purchases go through Stripe Managed Payments. Stripe is the seller of record and collects your payment details, email address and billing location to take the payment and work out sales tax. We never see your card details. How Stripe uses this is covered by Stripe's Privacy Policy.

Stripe then tells us about the purchase, and we keep a record of it: your email address, the amount and currency, the date and how many times you've downloaded the app. We use it to give you your download and to email you a new link when you ask for one. Those emails are sent through Resend, and the record is stored with Supabase.

We don't sell or share your information, and we don't use it for marketing.

The website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or ads, and its fonts are hosted here. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address to keep the service running and secure. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

How long we keep it

We keep your purchase record so you can download the app again later, and for our tax and accounting records. Emailed download links stop working after a day.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us from the address you paid with. We'll reply within 30 days. If we delete your purchase record, we won't be able to send you the app again, and we may keep what the law requires us to.

Children

Radial Keys isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email us at management@bywhatevermedium.com.
