Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information Endless Energy (“the app”, “we”) collects and how it is used. The short version: the game itself collects nothing about you, but it shows ads, and the ad provider collects some information to do that.

Information the game keeps

Your progress, settings and energy are saved only on your device. There are no accounts, and we don't send this information to ourselves or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Unity Ads, served through Unity LevelPlay, which may work with other ad networks to fill them. To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, Unity and those networks may collect:

  • Device information such as model, operating system version, language and screen size
  • Your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it
  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks
  • The app vendor identifier (IDFV)
  • Which ads you see and tap

How Unity uses this information is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks before any app can use your advertising identifier. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Personalised ads: if you turn tracking off, you will still see ads, but they won't be based on your activity in other apps.
  • Deleting your data: delete the app to remove everything stored on your device. For data held by Unity, see their privacy policy or contact them directly.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
