Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Get in touch.

Email management@bywhatevermedium.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working. We read every message.

How do I win a level?

Power every strip and light every lamp in its own colour, then unplug the generator. If everything stays lit, the level is solved. Strips wired into a loop keep each other powered.

How do cords work?

Tap a socket, then tap a free socket to run a cord between them; energy flows from the first to the second. Dragging works too. Tap either end of a cord to unplug it.

How do I get more energy for hints?

Beating a level for the first time pays energy: the closer you are to the fewest possible moves, the more you earn. Restarting a level gives up its energy. When you run out, you can watch a short ad for enough energy for one hint.

Does the game save my progress?

Yes, on your device. Progress isn't backed up online, so deleting the app resets it.
